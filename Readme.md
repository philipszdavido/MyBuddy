### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **MyBuddy – Smart Chat with Your Contacts**

**MyBuddy** is a powerful and elegant iOS chat app that brings you closer to your contacts with rich conversations and a personalized feed experience.

#### 💬 **Features:**

* **1-on-1 Chat**
  Chat instantly with your phone contacts using a sleek, SwiftUI-powered interface.

* **📸 Send & Receive Media**
  Share photos with your friends using secure, high-quality Cloudinary storage.

* **📥 Real-time Messaging**
  Messages are delivered and synced instantly using Firebase Firestore and Core Data for offline access.

* **📰 Personalized Feed System**
  View posts shared by your contacts. Enjoy a hybrid feed experience that’s scalable and personalized for your engagement level.

* **❤️ React to Posts**
  Like or dislike posts from your friends. Let them know what resonates with you.

---

#### ⚙️ **Built With:**

* **SwiftUI** for a modern, fluid iOS user experience
* **Core Data** for local message and media caching
* **Firebase Auth & Firestore** for secure authentication and real-time syncing
* **Cloudinary** for efficient and scalable media uploads
* **Background Cloud Functions** for scalable feed fan-out logic
